HotspotPlus Lite · Admin design · v1

Design rationale

A quiet tool for one person at a front desk. It should feel like a good phone settings app: calm, big touch targets, plain words. Nothing here needs training.

The idea in four lines

  • Calm. Light grey page, white rounded cards, thin lines, soft shadows. One blue accent. Colour is kept for meaning.
  • Quick jobs first. Add guest, print vouchers, see who is online, kick, check router. These are always one tap from the side bar (or the bottom bar on a phone).
  • Status is never only colour. Every status has a word and a shape: Active ✓, Expiring (clock), Expired / Over quota (warning), Disabled (slash). Online is a filled dot, offline a hollow ring, and both have text for screen readers.
  • Same markup everywhere. Each screen is one piece of HTML that adapts from 360 to 1920 px. Tables become stacked cards on a phone. The boards show 1280 and 390 side by side from the same markup.

Navigation

Side bar on a laptop (8 places, router status at the bottom). On a tablet it shrinks to icons. On a phone it becomes a bottom bar with 4 places plus “More”. The top bar always carries Password, the theme switch and Log out; on a phone these are three 44 px icon buttons.

Look and feel

  • System font stack (San Francisco on Apple, Segoe UI on Windows, Roboto on Android). 7-step type scale from 12 to 34 px.
  • Radius: 8 / 12 / 20 / 28 px and full pill. Buttons and chips are pills; cards are 20 px; sheets and dialogs 28 px.
  • Depth: three shadows. Top bar and side bar are slightly see-through with blur. Dark mode uses lighter surfaces instead of heavier shadows.
  • Motion: only 160 ms hover/press, a 200 ms sheet slide, and a soft fade when numbers refresh. Reduced-motion turns all of it off.

The 10-second refresh

The Dashboard swaps only the changed numbers. Tiles have a fixed height, numbers use fixed-width digits, and charts keep their size, so nothing jumps. New values fade from 55% to 100% in 0.4 s. A small “Live” dot with “Updated 4 s ago” shows it is alive.

What I assumed (tell me if any is wrong)

  • Business name sample: “Harbour Café”. Wi-Fi name sample: “Harbour-Guest”. All data on the boards is made up.
  • “Over quota” is shown as a red status, in the same family as Expired. The filter chip “Expired” includes it.
  • “Expiring soon” means ends within 3 days. Over quota means 100% of the data limit.
  • The Password page link in the top bar goes to a normal change-password page. I designed the forced “Set new password” screen only; the normal page reuses the same card.
  • Voucher sheet: A4, 3 × 6 = 18 vouchers per page, business name on top of the page.
  • Router page: eight MikroTik hotspot pages (login, alogin, status, logout, error, redirect, rlogin, radvert).
  • Phone “More” opens a small list: Plans, Usage, Router, Settings. It is a menu, not a page.
  • Icons are a small inline SVG sprite per page (24 px grid, 1.75 px stroke). No emoji, no remote files.
