    Wi-Fi accounts for your MikroTik hotspot, on your own network.

    Members, plans, vouchers and live sessions for hotels, cafes and small ISPs. One Go binary with SQLite inside. No cloud, no database server.

    Linux, 64-bit x86
    curl -LO https://github.com/pysf/HotspotPlusLight/releases/latest/download/hotspotpluslight-linux-amd64.tar.gz
    Windows, 64-bit (PowerShell)
    curl.exe -LO https://github.com/pysf/HotspotPlusLight/releases/latest/download/hotspotpluslight-windows-amd64.zip
    macOS, Apple silicon
    curl -LO https://github.com/pysf/HotspotPlusLight/releases/latest/download/hotspotpluslight-darwin-arm64.tar.gz

    Intel Macs, 32-bit and ARM builds, checksums and building from source: see the installation guide.

    Everything for Wi-Fi accounts. Nothing that needs care.

    Hand out accounts, set the limits, see who is online and disconnect anyone. The rest of the hotspot stays on your router.

    Members and vouchers

    Add a member one by one, or print a batch of vouchers on A4 sheets. You can always look up a member's password again.

    Plans

    Data, time, validity days, speed and number of devices. Define a plan once and give it to as many members as you like.

    20 GB30 days10 Mbps2 devices

    Limits that hold

    The router gets the limits at login. The server also watches the usage and disconnects a member who goes over.

    Your login page

    8 designs to download. Change the text and logo by hand, and translate the messages.

    Runs anywhere

    A Windows service, Linux or macOS. 9 builds, including 32-bit and 64-bit ARM.

    1binary to deploy
    1SQLite file is your whole database
    30,000online users tested

    The test used a simulated router on one machine. Your router may be the limit, not the server. See the performance section of the README.

    HotspotPlus Lite is a small rewrite of HotspotPlus. It keeps the member, plan and quota rules and drops the multi-tenant product around them.

    Ready to try it?

    Install the binary, open the admin panel, then follow the router setup.
