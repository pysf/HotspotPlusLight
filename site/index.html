    open source · self-hosted · AGPL-3.0

    Wi-Fi accounts for your MikroTik hotspot, on your own network.

    Members, vouchers, plans and live sessions. One program and one SQLite file. No cloud, no database server.

    Linux, 64-bit x86
    curl -LO https://github.com/pysf/HotspotPlusLight/releases/latest/download/hotspotpluslight-linux-amd64.tar.gz
    Windows, 64-bit (PowerShell)
    curl.exe -LO https://github.com/pysf/HotspotPlusLight/releases/latest/download/hotspotpluslight-windows-amd64.zip
    macOS, Apple silicon
    curl -LO https://github.com/pysf/HotspotPlusLight/releases/latest/download/hotspotpluslight-darwin-arm64.tar.gz

    Intel Macs, 32-bit and ARM builds, checksums and building from source: see Installation.

    What it does

    Create accounts, set limits, see who is online and disconnect any member. The Dashboard shows the state of the hotspot.

    Members and vouchers

    Add members one by one, or print a batch of vouchers on A4. You can look up any password later.

    Plans

    Data limit, time limit, validity period, speed limit and simultaneous devices. One plan, many members.

    20 GB30 days10 Mbps2 devices

    Limits that hold

    The router gets the limits at login. The server also disconnects a member who goes over.

    Your login page

    8 designs to download. Edit the text and logo, and translate the messages.

    Runs anywhere

    A Windows service, or Linux and macOS. 9 builds, including 32-bit and 64-bit ARM.

    1binary to deploy
    1SQLite file, the whole database
    30,000online users tested

    Tested with a simulated router on one machine. Your router may be the limit. See the performance section

    A small rewrite of HotspotPlus for one router. It keeps the same rules for members, plans and limits.
